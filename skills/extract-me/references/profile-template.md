# PROFILE.md shape

The headings below are a contract: other skills look them up by name. Keep them exact.

```markdown
# Expertise profile: <display name>
updated: YYYY-MM-DD · scan: <projects> projects, <repos> repos (<repos_mine> with own commits, <commits> commits), <files> files, <skills_authored> skills authored

## Summary
Two or three sentences: what this person builds, for whom, and at what scale.

## Strengths
1. **<Domain>: <plain claim>.** Evidence: `<path>` (<count>), `<path>` (<count>), skill `<name>`. See `<domain>.md`.
2. ...

## Signature methods
- **<method name>**: what it is in one line. Seen in: `<path>`, `<path>`.

## Active work (last 30 days)
- <category>: `<path>` (last touched YYYY-MM-DD), ...

## Working style
- <observable pattern>. Seen in: <where>.

## Also seen
- <domain>: one line, why it is below the bar.

## Gaps and blind spots
- What the scan cannot see (other machines, cloud tools, meetings) and what that means for the claims.
```

Under 120 lines. No secrets, no other people's private data.
